# DMT: Investor Pitch Deck

## Executive Summary

**DMT** is a next-generation AI-powered CRM ecosystem designed specifically for dental medical tourism clinics. We're solving a $40 billion global market problem with a $3-5 billion opportunity in Turkey alone.

### The Problem

Turkey receives 1M+ medical tourists annually, with dental tourism representing $3-5B in revenue. Yet clinics operate with:
- Fragmented, outdated systems
- Generic CRMs not designed for dental workflows
- No multilingual AI support
- Disconnected patient journeys
- Poor compliance automation
- No intelligent logistics coordination
- Limited financial yield management

Current solutions (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho) are generic, expensive, and lack:
- Dental-specific workflows
- Medical tourism coordination
- AI diagnostics integration
- Turkish regulatory compliance
- Multilingual AI communication

### Our Solution

**DMT** is the operating system for dental medical tourism:
- Unified patient journey orchestration
- AI-powered diagnostics and communication
- Native Turkish compliance (KVKK, Ministry of Health)
- International travel coordination
- Advanced financial management
- Modern, beautiful interface

### Market Opportunity

- **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**: $40B (global dental tourism)
- **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)**: $5B (Turkey + regional)
- **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**: $500M (Year 5 projection)
